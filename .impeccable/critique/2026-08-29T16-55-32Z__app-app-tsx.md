## Design Specificity Verdict

The homepage is authored for a Persian steel-pricing business, not a category-interchangeable template. Real steel photography, black/bronze/yellow materials, industrial type scale, live-price language, and the dispatch-board overview create genuine product character. The refractive Liquid Glass treatment is an asset and should remain rich.

Specificity weakens after the hero because the page falls into a familiar sequence: large card grid, search, tabs, table, generic conversion block. The biggest opportunity is to make the information architecture as purpose-built as the material treatment.

## Overall Impression

The site opens with authority and eventually delivers a credible market instrument, but it makes buyers work too hard to reach that instrument. The single biggest opportunity is to turn the dispatch-board price overview into the homepage's organizing spine and demote repeated product-family navigation to supporting discovery.

## What's Working

1. **A convincing steel-specific visual world.** Real imagery, metallic darkness, controlled yellow light, refractive surfaces, and industrial scale feel bespoke and trustworthy.
2. **A credible overview table.** Products, ranges, units, status badges, thumbnails, and direct links behave like a real market instrument rather than marketing decoration.
3. **Strong conversion availability.** Full-width mobile hero actions and the persistent bottom contact/quote bar keep high-value actions reachable.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] The price CTA lands before a 4,910 px mobile category wall

- **What:** `CategoryGrid` sits between the price anchor and the actual search/overview. At 390×844, `#prices` begins near y=1,111, the category grid spans about 4,910 px, search begins near y=6,428, and the overview starts near y=6,577.
- **Why it matters:** “ورود به مرکز قیمت فولاد” promises direct price discovery but requires roughly seven more mobile screens before showing prices.
- **Fix:** Land the CTA on the overview; move search/overview above the editorial gallery; make category cards compact, correctly constrained image tiles rather than ~600 px-tall blocks.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`

### [P1] Market-feed failure dominates the first post-hero moment

- **What:** The live session showed “نرخ‌های بازار در حال حاضر در دسترس نیست” immediately below the hero in a showcase-scale band.
- **Why it matters:** It creates a trust valley directly after the site promises current prices.
- **Fix:** Collapse failure into a slim status rail, show last successful update when available, add retry, and lead directly into authoritative steel-price content.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P2] Product-family selection is presented three times

- **What:** Buyers encounter eight large category cards, eight tabs, and eight overview rows representing overlapping choices.
- **Why it matters:** It increases decision fatigue and makes users wonder which selector is authoritative.
- **Fix:** Use one primary model: search plus a compact family selector attached to the dispatch-board overview. Move the large image gallery later as editorial discovery or remove it.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P2] Mobile tabs hide most choices without a clear affordance

- **What:** At 390 px only about three tabs are visible; the remaining five sit off-canvas without an obvious horizontal-scroll cue.
- **Why it matters:** First-time and distracted users may assume only three product groups exist.
- **Fix:** Remove the redundant rail, or use wrapping compact chips/a labeled selector. If horizontal scrolling remains, add a visible clipped edge/fade and accessible scroll affordance.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

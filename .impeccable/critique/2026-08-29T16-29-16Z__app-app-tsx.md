#### Overall Impression
A highly credible, robust industrial steel trading platform with exceptional Persian typography and rich domain-specific data structures. Refining the dark-to-light card contrast consistency and eliminating layout jank during scroll will elevate it to true director-grade polish.

#### What's Working
1. **Domain-True Data Display**: The steel price overview tables and catalog roving tabs display factory brands, grades, sizing, and price trends with natural industry clarity and tabular numeral precision.
2. **Atmospheric Brand Palette**: The combination of `#090c0f` deep steel, frosted glass tokens, and `#f6b500` warm gold accents creates an authoritative, modern industrial presence.
3. **Persian Typography & Accessibility**: Dedicated handling of RTL flow, `dir="rtl"`, B Titr bold Persian branding, skip links, and roving tab index keyboard navigation.

#### Priority Issues
- **[P1] Visual Inconsistency Between Light Category Cards and Dark Steel Shell**:
  - **What**: Category cards (`.category-card`) feature solid white backgrounds (`#ffffff`), clashing sharply with the surrounding dark liquid steel section (`#3b3b3e` / `#090c0f`).
  - **Why it matters**: Creates glaring contrast dissonance and undermines the unified premium dark aesthetic.
  - **Fix**: Re-theme category cards using dark glass tokens (`--glass-dark-bg`, `--surface-border`, `--surface-feature`) with warm gold hover accents.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable colorize`

- **[P1] Performance Layout Thrash in Header Resizing**:
  - **What**: Animating `height` and `min-height` transitions on `.header-bar-inner`, `.brand-mark-img`, and `.header-nav-inner` during scroll/compact states (`header.css:52, 76, 241`).
  - **Why it matters**: Layout property transitions force continuous CPU layout reflows during scroll, causing noticeable jank on mobile and low-powered screens.
  - **Fix**: Refactor header compact state to use discrete threshold classes or GPU-accelerated CSS `transform: scale()` / `opacity`.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable optimize`

- **[P1] Search Bar Hierarchy & Discoverability**:
  - **What**: The search bar is nestled below the hero and category grid, requiring users to scroll into the section to perform quick product lookups.
  - **Why it matters**: Contractors and frequent buyers needing instant price lookups face unnecessary friction before accessing the search input.
  - **Fix**: Elevate search into a more prominent position alongside the category navigation or integrate quick-filter directly into the price catalog header.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`

- **[P2] Mobile Mega-Menu Stacking Fatigue**:
  - **What**: On viewports < 900px, the mega menu expands all subcategories, factory names, and diameter sizes into one very tall vertical list.
  - **Why it matters**: Requires extensive scrolling on mobile devices to reach lower navigation links or the quote action button.
  - **Fix**: Implement collapsible accordion sections or segmented sub-tabs within the mobile menu drawer.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable adapt`

- **[P2] Repetitive Side-Tab Accent Borders**:
  - **What**: Multiple badges and headings use `border-inline-start: 4px/5px solid var(--brand-yellow)` and `border-top: 3px solid` across hero, header, and guides.
  - **Why it matters**: Adds visual clutter and clashes with rounded card borders.
  - **Fix**: Replace heavy linear borders with subtle background pill badges, typography weight hierarchy, or gold text accents.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable polish`
